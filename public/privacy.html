Privacy Policy

Vaibik (“we”, “the app”) is a learning app and website (vaibik.site) operated by Aidar Altynbek. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it. Contact: support@vaibik.site.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and an internal user ID, used to create and secure your account (hosted by Supabase).
  • Learning progress: lessons completed, points, streaks, tokens, quests, leaderboard league and the display name you choose.
  • Homework text: answers you submit for AI review are sent to DeepSeek’s API to generate feedback. Don’t include personal information in homework.
  • Feedback you send us voluntarily.
  • Usage analytics: how features are used (screens viewed, lessons started), via PostHog, to improve the product.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance data via Sentry, to fix bugs.
  • Payments: on the web, subscriptions are processed by Polar; on iOS, by Apple. We never see your full card details; we only receive subscription status.

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites and do not use data for third-party advertising.

Service providers

Supabase (accounts and database), DeepSeek (AI homework review), PostHog (analytics), Sentry (diagnostics), Vercel (hosting), Polar and Apple (payments). They process data only to provide their services to us.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request access to or deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing support@vaibik.site from your account email; we delete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep records (e.g. payment records). Analytics and diagnostic data are kept for a limited period and then deleted or aggregated.

Children

Vaibik is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date below shows the latest version.

Чего мы не делаем

  • Не продаём персональные данные.
  • Не отслеживаем вас в чужих приложениях и сайтах и не используем данные для сторонней рекламы.

Хранение и удаление

Данные хранятся, пока активен аккаунт. Запрос на доступ или удаление — на support@vaibik.site с email аккаунта; удаляем в течение 30 дней, кроме записей, которые требуется хранить по закону.
